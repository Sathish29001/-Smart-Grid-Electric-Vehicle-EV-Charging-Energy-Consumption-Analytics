Excited to share my latest end-to-end data analytics project exploring EV charging telemetry, grid demand patterns, and consumer behavior using Python!

Key Highlights & Methodology:

Data Wrangling & Feature Engineering: Cleaned 1,200+ multi-city charging records, handled missing ambient temperature telemetry via median imputation, and engineered custom datetime features (charging duration, day of week, peak hours).

Advanced Pandas & NumPy Analysis: Grouped multi-level user tiers and charging speeds, computed cumulative energy sums by city, and leveraged NumPy vectorization to calculate efficiency percentiles and correlation matrices.

Executive Data Storytelling: Built 7+ custom Matplotlib and Seaborn visualizations, including time-series trends, hourly heatmaps, and box/violin distributions to uncover key grid insights.

Key Findings:

Energy demand heavily peaks during evening hours (18:00–22:00) on weekdays.
Fleet users maintain highly optimized, rapid turnaround charging cycles compared to standard consumers.

Identified station variance hotspots signaling mixed vehicle compatibility or hardware wear.
